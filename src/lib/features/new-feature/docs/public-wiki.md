Here is an example of a public wiki help page for purchasing a course. Feel free to add images, videos, or other media to help explain the feature.

## Paying for Courses

[Some courses are free, but many are paid. If a course is paid, the user can enroll in the course by clicking the 'enroll' button. If the user does not have a saved payment method, they will be redirected to a payment form to enter their payment method. The user will be taken back to the course page after enrollment.]

## Why are some courses paid?

[Some courses are paid to cover the cost of creating and maintaining the course. This helps us keep the courses free for everyone. Users directly pay the administrator of the course.]

## How do I pay for a course?

[If you are logged in, you can pay for a course by clicking the 'enroll' button. If you are not logged in, you will be redirected to a login page. After logging in, you will be redirected to the payment form. You can pay for the course by entering your payment method and clicking the 'pay' button. You will be taken back to the course page after payment.]

## How do I cancel a course?

You can cancel a course by clicking the 'cancel' button. You will be taken back to the course page after cancellation.

## How do I refund a course?

You can refund a course by clicking the 'refund' button. You will be taken back to the course page after refund.
